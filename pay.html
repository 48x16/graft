What a week's work pays, by job and by region, before inflation.

A week's pay

The Annual Survey of Hours and Earnings (ASHE) counts employee jobs, so someone with two jobs counts twice. It covers the UK in April each year. PAYE Real Time Information covers payrolled employees only (no self-employed), mixes full-time and part-time work, and its latest month is a flash estimate that will be revised. All pay is gross and before inflation.

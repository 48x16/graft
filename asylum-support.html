The region

Cheap housing, more support

The pattern follows the cost of housing. Home Office contractors buy the accommodation, and they are paid set rates, so they make more profit where housing is cheaper. That is why it has concentrated in cheaper areas, the Home Affairs Committee found in October 2025. Councils do not choose where it goes.

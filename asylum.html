Asylum Claims

  • Data Home Office, immigration system statistics
  • Covers Asylum claims, initial decisions, claims awaiting a decision and asylum support, UK
  • Updated the latest quarter

Claims, decisions and the queue in between, quarter by quarter.

Claims

People claiming asylum in the UK, at port or after entry, in each year ending June. People are main applicants plus their dependants; the count of people starts when dependants were first recorded. The decisions made in a year do not relate to that year's claims.

People claiming asylum, each year ending June

The queue

Claims awaiting an initial decision at the end of each quarter, UK. Appeals and further reviews are left out throughout. People include dependants; cases are main applicants. Waiting time is counted from the date of the claim.

Awaiting an initial decision, at each quarter end

How long they had waited, latest quarter

Grant rate

Initial decisions on main applicants (cases), not people, UK. The grant rate is grants of protection or other leave as a share of grants and refusals; withdrawals and administrative outcomes are left out. Years with fewer than 100 grants and refusals are not shown. Appeals can change outcomes and are not shown.

Share of initial decisions that were grants, each year ending June

By nationality

The ten most common nationalities by number of people claiming asylum in the latest year ending June, ordered by claims, as published by the Home Office ("Other and unknown" left out). Grant rates are on initial decisions on main applicants in the same year, not necessarily the same people; a rate is shown only where there were at least 50 grants and refusals.

People claiming asylum, by nationality

On support

People receiving Home Office asylum support (Sections 95, 4 and 98) at the end of each quarter, UK, including dependants. Hotels are contingency accommodation. Recent figures come from a new case system and are provisional. Unaccompanied children supported by local authorities are not included. For one area, see Support by Area.
